# Color Theory — Palettes by sector

## Fundamentals

### Contrast and readability
- WCAG AA ratio: 4.5:1 minimum for body text, 3:1 for titles > 18pt
- Never use #000000 as text color — a tinted dark gray (e.g. #2C1810 for warm, #1A1A2E for cool) reads more elegantly
- bg must never tire the eyes — avoid pure white (#FFF) for printed documents, prefer an off-white

### Harmony
- **Monochrome**: lightness variations of a single hue. Safe, elegant, never misses
- **Complementary**: two opposite hues on the color wheel. Strong contrast, use sparingly
- **Analogous**: 2–3 neighboring hues. Harmonious, natural
- **Triadic**: 3 colors 120° apart. Vibrant — for events or youth audiences

### Extraction from photos
When the project has images, the palette should derive from them:
1. `maket_image view` the key photos
2. Identify the dominant color → candidate for `bg` or `primary`
3. Identify the natural accent color → `accent`
4. Text must contrast with the dominant

## Colors to avoid

- **Green + red** as primary combo — colorblindness
- **Yellow on white** — unreadable
- **Pure blue (#0000FF)** — harsh, amateurish
- **Over-saturation** — visual fatigue, feels "cheap"
- **Mid-gray (#808080)** as text — insufficient contrast
